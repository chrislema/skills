# Reference file template

Fill every section. Use the person's words. Where a section has nothing, write what's missing rather than inventing content. Keep the core/follow-up distinction in the questions: a follow-up is asked only when its condition applies.

```markdown
# Decision guide: [the decision, in their words]

**Owner:** [name, role] · **Version:** 1 · **Last corrected:** [date]

## The decision
[One or two sentences, their phrasing: what's being decided, and what typically triggers it.]

## How often and who else touches it
[Frequency, who brings it to them, who's affected, who covers when they're out.]

## The questions I ask
Core (always):
- [question]
- [question]

Follow-ups (only when the condition applies):
- [question]: *only if [condition]*
- [question]: *only if [condition]*

## The rules I hold
- [rule, verbatim]: *bends when:* [exception, or "no exceptions given"]
- [rule, verbatim]: *bends when:* [exception]

## Sure yes
- [what makes it an instant yes, as specific as they got]

## Sure no
- [what makes it an instant no]

## The yes I'd regret
- [what looks like a yes but isn't, and the tell]

## The no I'd regret
- [what I might wrongly turn down, and the tell]

## Scoring the hard part
**Question:** [plain question]
- **1:** [anchor]
- **5:** [anchor]
- **10:** [anchor]

## Stress tests and my calls
1. **[Regret trap, one line]** → [their call] because [their reason]. *Matches guide:* yes / no → [what was updated]
2. **[Near miss]** → ...
3. **[Collision]** → ...

## Corrections log
What the first, neat version got wrong. This is where the real judgment showed up.
- First said: "[neat version]" → Corrected to: "[their correction]"
- ...

## Open gaps
- [anything unresolved, skipped, or still vague, and what would settle it]

## How to use this guide
**For a person covering for me:** Start with the core questions. Sure-yes and sure-no cases you can decide. Anything near a threshold, matching a regret pattern, or where two rules collide: bring it to [owner].

**As AI instructions:** Use this guide to check a case against my questions, rules, and thresholds. Point out which questions are unanswered, which rules apply, and whether the case matches a sure-yes, a sure-no, or a regret pattern. Score the hard part with the rubric above and show the reasoning. Do not make the final call. Flag borderline cases back to [owner], and never fill a gap in this guide with your own judgment.
```

# Project definition template

Fill every section. Use the client's own words wherever possible. Write only what the client said or confirmed. If something is unknown, say so in "Still open" instead of guessing. One page.

```markdown
# Project definition: [short name for the project, in their words]

**Prepared for:** [agency name, if known] · **From:** [client name or company] · **Date:** [date]

## What we're doing
[Two or three sentences, their phrasing. What the project is and why now.]

## The goal
[What a win looks like, in their words. Specific if they got specific.]

## What would be a miss
[What a loss looks like. What they'd regret.]

## Who it's for
[The audience or users. Who sees it, who uses it.]

## Where it shows up
[Every place it has to work. Include the ones that came out late in the conversation; those are the ones agencies usually miss.]

## What's in and what's out
**In:**
- [thing]
**Out (for now):**
- [thing]

## What's been tried before
[What they've done, and what didn't work, and why, as they described it.]

## What they're most excited about
[Their words.]

## What they love and what they don't want
- Love: [examples or reasons]
- Don't want: [examples or reasons]

## Who decides, and when
[Decision-makers, approvals, deadline, budget range if given.]

## Still open
- [anything unresolved, unknown, or still vague, and who would settle it]
```
